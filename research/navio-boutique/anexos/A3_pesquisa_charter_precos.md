**Two pricing structures.** The same ship shows up in two forms, and the plan should model them separately:
1. **Variety's own cruise-style charter** (sold direct or via Goolets): the fee includes half board, crew, and fuel for 6 h/day. Taxes, VAT, the second meal, drinks and tips are added on top.
2. **The broker MYBA-style rate** ("+ expenses"): this is a higher headline fee, plus an APA of 30–40% and VAT.

## 2. Effective all-in charter cost per week (ESTIMATES)

**Shared method and assumptions:**
- **Meals:** Variety charters include half board. I add the second main meal at **€50–60 pp/day** (PREMISE; Variety's "Food Options" price list was not visible).
- **Drinks:** wine/beer/soft drinks package at **€40–60 pp/day** (PREMISE).
- **Tips:** **€15–18 pp/day** (Goolets).
- **Excursions:** **€60–100 pp/day for 5 port days** (PREMISE).
- **VAT:** 12% in Greece, 13% in Croatia.
- **Port charges:** Variety's own figures ($490 pp, or €330–345 pp).
- **Occupancy:** double occupancy at full capacity.

## 4. Seasonality and winter deployment
- **Greece, Variety charter seasons** (Galileo 2024, CONFIRMED – goolets):

  | Months | Weekly fee | Level |
  |---|---|---|
  | Mar–Apr and Nov | €92.0k | Lowest |
  | Jul–Aug and Oct | €121.9k | Mid |
  | May–Jun and Sep | €166.7k | Highest |

  The **peak charter months are the shoulder months**. July and August are cheaper to charter (Meltemi winds and heat). The low/high spread is **≈ 1.8×**. For the broker rates, the spread is ≈ 1.34–1.38× (Voyager 247.65 → 342; Harmony V 129.4 → 172.9).
- **Croatia:** the season is roughly April to October (Katarina sailings run Apr–Oct 2026). Karizma's 2027 rates differ by only **±7%** between low, mid and high season (84 / 89 / 95k).
- **Retail:** Emerald Azzurra fares rise from $6,984 (1 Aug) to $10,515 (22 Aug) and $10,821 (10 Oct). Variety's August 2026 fares are about 30–45% above its October 2027 "from" fares.
- **Variety winter deployment** (CONFIRMED – taoticket catalogues and travelpulse):
  - **Seychelles** (M/Y Pegasus): 7 n from Mahé; 2027 departures 2 Jan, 6 Feb, 20 Mar, 4/18/25 Dec.
  - **Cape Verde** (Harmony V).
  - **Rivers of West Africa**, Dakar to Dakar.
  - **Costa Rica & Panama / Panama Canal** (Panorama).
- **Repositioning:** no published cost was found. **ESTIMATE:**
  - **Piraeus to Cape Verde/Dakar:** about 2,700–3,000 nm, or about **12–14 days at 9–10 kn**.
  - **Piraeus to Seychelles via Suez:** about 3,600 nm, or about **15–17 days**, plus Suez fees and a security premium for the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.
  - **Piraeus to Costa Rica (Atlantic crossing):** about 5,500+ nm, or about **3–4 weeks**.
  - An owner should budget **2–4 lost revenue weeks per round trip, plus fuel**. This is why charter (where the operator carries this cost) favours Mediterranean-season-only programmes.
- **Red Sea / Egypt and Caribbean alternatives:** SeaDream's Caribbean charter is quoted at ~$630k/wk, against ~$950k in the Med (CONFIRMED – Mundy). That is a **≈ 34% winter discount** at the ultra-luxury end.

- **Demand for charter:** SeaDream runs **up to 25%** of its sailings as private charters (CONFIRMED – travelagentcentral). Katarina Line and Unforgettable Croatia report that small-ship charters are growing in popularity. Unforgettable Croatia's "from" price went from **$95k to $160k** per week (CONFIRMED – travelpulse / cruisetradenews).
- **Charter fee vs. full-ship retail.** Variety Voyager at retail "from" $3,060–4,250 pp × 72 gives ≈ €200k–278k of full-ship revenue per week. The charter fee is €194k–342k plus extras. **ESTIMATE:** the whole-ship charter fee therefore runs about **1.0–1.3× a full ship sold at "from" fares**. The operator is pricing in the risk of unsold cabins and the discounting it would otherwise need (it advertises 20–50% sales).
- **Themed premium.** Luxury small-ship products with gastronomy or culture content sell at **€700–2,100 per night** (Sea Cloud, Emerald, SeaDream, Culture Discovery), against €350–600 for Variety cruise-only. **ESTIMATE / PREMISE:** a curated QIMO programme (wine, gastronomy, guides, private events) can plausibly sell at **1.5–2.5× the underlying charter cost per pax**. No direct study of price elasticity was found.
